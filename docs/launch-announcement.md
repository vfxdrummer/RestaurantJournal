## LinkedIn (founder story — the flagship)

Today I launched **Restaurant Journal**. 🍽️

It started with a simple frustration: I have thousands of photos from meals out — birthdays, first dates, that hole-in-the-wall taco place — and no way to remember where any of them actually were.

So I built an app that fixes it. Restaurant Journal scans the photos you *already have* and quietly turns them into a private map of everywhere you've eaten. No typing. No check-ins. No account. Your photos never leave your phone.

Open it and your whole dining life is laid out — every city, every neighborhood, every place worth remembering. Rate the meals (😋 Yay / 🙂 Okay / 😐 Meh), add a voice note, and rediscover spots you'd forgotten you loved.

Built private by design, for anyone who believes a good meal is a memory worth keeping.

Free on the App Store for iPhone → [App Store link]

**Eat. Experience. Remember.**
